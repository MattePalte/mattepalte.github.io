I am a Research Scientist at Meta, where I work on AI research. I completed my PhD at University of Stuttgart, part of TU9 (the alliance of leading Technical Universities in Germany), advised by Prof. Dr. Michael Pradel. My main research focus is at the intersection of software engineering and quantum computing, with the aim of increasing the reliability of the quantum software stack. In addition to my focus on quantum computing, I also love to explore the application of Explainable AI for models of code.

Experience

My Ph.D. research also benefits from previous complementary experiences as a summer research intern at Meta in the Core AI team, a summer research intern at GitHub Next in the GitHub Copilot team, where I applied Explainable AI to code exploration, and as a research intern at CERN, before my Ph.D., where I developed a cloud anomaly detection system at CERN Cloud Team. I completed my Double M.Sc. in Computer Science Engineering (specialization in Data Science) at Politecnico di Milano and Technical University of Berlin as a part of EIT Digital Master School. I am naturally curious and I love to face challenging situations that make me grow.

Bio in 3rd Person

Suggested for conference or invited talks.
Matteo Paltenghi is a Research Scientist at Meta with expertise at the intersection of artificial intelligence and software engineering. He completed his Ph.D. at University of Stuttgart, advised by Prof. Dr. sc. Michael Pradel, with work spanning software engineering, AI, and quantum computing, and presentations at top conferences like ASE 21, OOPSLA 22, ICSE 23, ICSE 24, FSE 24, and EMNLP 24. During his Ph.D., he interned at GitHub Next, harnessing Large Language Models for code exploration (TSE 2024), and at Meta's Core AI team, working on scaling parameter-constrained language models (EMNLP 2024). Prior to his Ph.D., he spent 9 months at CERN for his Master Thesis on anomaly detection on data centers. Matteo holds a double degree M.Sc. Computer Science and Engineering from Politecnico di Milano and TU Berlin, preceded by a B.Sc. from Politecnico di Milano. He serves as reviewer for TOSEM, TSE, ISSTA and JSS, and was selected as a young researcher for the Heidelberg Laureate Forum (HLF 23).
